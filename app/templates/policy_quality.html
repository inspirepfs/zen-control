How to read this

Not every finding is an error. Shadows and redundancy often represent intentional precedence; ZEN makes them visible so the effective design stays understandable.

WARNINGIntent exists but a dependency or enforcement condition is inactive/missing.
SHADOWA more-specific control deterministically wins over a broader control.
REDUNDANTConfiguration duplicates an outcome and may be unnecessary.
UNUSEDSaved configuration currently has no managed target/effect.
